### Active Shielding Sensitivity

Preliminary active magnetic deflection at Seed Vessel scale is estimated to save 200–400 t of passive mass at the cost of significant continuous power and coil mass. Decision deferred until structural and power budgets are tighter; pure passive + dual-use water remains the current baseline.

## 4. Coordination with Structural Results

- Water tanks contribute both radiation protection and structural mass (already partially accounted in deck/internal structure).
- The 450–750 t dedicated shielding is the key new number to fold into the reconciled mass budget alongside the CFRP structural saving.

## 5. Recommendation for PATSAGi

Adopt the **optimised dual-use water + 450–750 t dedicated shielding** configuration as the baseline for the next mass-budget revision. This meets the provisional dose targets with acceptable mass.

Final dose limits and any active-shielding decision remain subject to TOLC 8 crew-safety valence review.
