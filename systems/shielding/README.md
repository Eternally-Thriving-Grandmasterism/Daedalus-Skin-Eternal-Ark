# Radiation & Particle Shielding

## Threats
- Galactic cosmic rays (GCR)
- Solar particle events (when near stars)
- Interstellar dust and gas impacts at high cruise velocity
- Secondary radiation from shielding materials themselves

## Multi-Layer Strategy
1. **Passive mass shielding**: Hydrogen-rich materials (water, polyethylene, specialized composites) placed optimally around habitats
2. **Active magnetic shielding**: Superconducting coils generating fields that deflect charged particles
3. **Forward particle shield**: Dedicated sacrificial / deflecting structure at the bow for high-speed interstellar medium impacts
4. **Storm shelters**: High-shielding core areas for solar particle events or high-GCR periods

## Integration with Daedalus-Skin
The outer self-healing layers and structural mass contribute to overall shielding. Water storage and life support tanks are deliberately positioned to serve dual roles as radiation barriers.
