# DSA-Ref-A Structure & Shielding — Refined Guidance

**Status:** Refined baseline  
**Reference Design:** DSA-Ref-A (spin radius ~140 m, ~300 people)  
**Owner:** Autonomicity Games Inc.  
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-11

## 1. Structural Priorities

- Primary rotating habitat must deliver 0.85–1.0 g at the main deck with acceptable Coriolis levels.
- Structural mass fraction is one of the highest-leverage uncertainties in the overall mass budget.
- Counter-rotating elements are preferred when mass and complexity allow, to manage net angular momentum.

## 2. Shielding Strategy for the Seed Vessel

- Maximize dual-use of life-support water as radiation shielding.
- Apply higher areal density to storm shelters and primary living zones.
- Use active magnetic shielding to reduce required passive mass where power and coil mass trade positively.
- Forward particle/dust shield remains mandatory for cruise at 0.05 c class speeds.

## 3. Mass Allocation Guidance (from overall budget)

Structure + rotating habitat and passive shielding together are expected to dominate dry mass. Design effort should focus on:

- Efficient load paths for spin-induced forces
- Minimizing non-structural mass in the rotating section
- Intelligent placement of water and stores for dual shielding duty
- Inspectable and repairable primary structure (aided by Daedalus-Skin outer layers)

## 4. Open High-Impact Analyses

- Finite-element level mass estimates for a 140 m class rotating habitat
- Areal density vs. dose curves for the reference mission duration
- Active vs. passive shielding trade for the Seed Vessel power budget
- Bearing, seal, and transfer system mass and reliability

---

**Structural and shielding mass remain the largest dry-mass uncertainties for DSA-Ref-A and are top priority for detailed modeling.**
