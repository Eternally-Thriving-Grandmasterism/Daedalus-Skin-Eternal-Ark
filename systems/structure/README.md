# Structure & Daedalus-Skin

## Hull Philosophy
Multi-layer, modular, self-healing, radiation-aware structure designed for multi-century service life.

### Layers (conceptual)
1. Outer sacrificial / self-healing skin (polymers, vitrimers, biological agents)
2. Structural load-bearing composites / alloys
3. Hydrogen-rich radiation shielding (water, polyethylene, or specialized composites)
4. Internal pressure vessel / habitat walls
5. Active magnetic shielding coils for charged particles

## Self-Healing ("Daedalus-Skin")
Inherited from AlphaProMega Air Foundation concepts:
- Microcapsule-based healing
- Vitrimer / reversible polymer networks
- Radiation-triggered or oxygen-triggered repair mechanisms
- Potential biological (bacterial / fungal) assisted repair for certain layers
