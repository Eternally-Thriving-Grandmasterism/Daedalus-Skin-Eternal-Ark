# Closed-Loop Life Support — Mass Balance Framework

**Status:** Preliminary framework  
**Owner:** Autonomicity Games Inc.

## Design Goal
Achieve near-closed loop (high recovery percentages) for multi-decade to multi-century missions with growth margin.

## Key Flows

### Atmosphere
- Human metabolic CO₂ production ≈ 1 kg/person/day
- O₂ consumption ≈ 0.84 kg/person/day
- Primary recovery: algal photobioreactors + higher plants
- Backup: physicochemical CO₂ scrubbers + O₂ generation

### Water
- Total water throughput high; recovery target > 98–99%
- Sources: urine, humidity condensate, hygiene, food production
- Multi-stage processing (filtration, distillation, catalytic, biological)

### Food & Biomass
- Caloric and nutritional requirements sized for target population + growth
- Mix of fast-cycle algae, leafy greens, and higher-calorie crops (potatoes, wheat, soy, etc.)
- Waste biomass returned to nutrient loops

## Scaling Notes
Population size strongly drives volume, power, and mass of the life support system. Early design will target a reference population (e.g. 10,000–25,000) with clear scaling factors for larger configurations.
