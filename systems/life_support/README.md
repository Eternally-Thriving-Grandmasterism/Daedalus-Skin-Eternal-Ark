# Life Support & Closed-Loop Ecology

## Primary Approach
Fully regenerative Biological Life Support System (BLSS) combined with physicochemical backup.

### Key Components
- High-density photobioreactors (*Chlorella vulgaris* and cyanobacteria strains)
- Higher plant cultivation (dwarf wheat, potatoes, soy, leafy greens, etc.)
- Water recovery (urine, condensate, grey water → potable)
- Waste processing and nutrient recycling
- Atmosphere management (CO₂ scrubbing + O₂ generation)

## Design Targets
- Near-closed loop for multi-decade to multi-century missions
- Food production sufficient for target population + growth margin
- Psychological support through living green spaces and variety
