# DSA-Ref-A — Magnetic Sail Performance Estimates

**Status:** Higher-fidelity planning estimates  
**Reference Design:** DSA-Ref-A (cruise ~0.055 c)  
**Owner:** Autonomicity Games Inc.  
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-11

## 1. Performance Goal

Decelerate from ~0.055 c to interplanetary injection speeds in a time that is operationally acceptable (target: years to low tens of years) while keeping sail mass inside the allocated 200–800 t range.

## 2. Governing Physics (Simplified)

Magnetic sail drag force scales with:
- Magnetic moment (or effective magnetospheric size)
- Relative velocity
- Local plasma density and velocity distribution

Because interstellar plasma density is low (~0.1 cm⁻³ typical order of magnitude, with significant variation), large effective field volumes are required for useful thrust (drag).

## 3. Design Implications for DSA-Ref-A

- A single large superconducting loop or a distributed array is required.
- Sail mass is dominated by conductor, structural support, and thermal/power systems rather than “fabric.”
- Deployment reliability and dynamic stability are as important as raw drag performance.
- The ship must retain residual fusion propellant as a backup and for final maneuvers.

## 4. Current Performance Stance

Until high-fidelity plasma simulations are available, the program assumes:

- The magnetic sail provides the majority of the deceleration Δv.
- Deceleration time may still be measured in years; mission timelines must accept this.
- Sail mass remains a major uncertainty and a top technology maturation item.
- Hybrid operation (sail + residual reverse thrust) is the robust baseline.

## 5. Required Higher-Fidelity Work

- Plasma drag models across realistic interstellar density ranges
- Magnetic moment vs. drag force curves at 0.03–0.07 c
- Structural and thermal design of the conductor system
- Deployment, attitude control, and failure-mode analysis
- Updated mass allocation once performance is better quantified

**Magnetic sail performance remains one of the largest remaining uncertainties for the Seed Vessel mission timeline and mass budget.**
