## 3. Recommendation

**Confirm the 550–750 t planning value for MS-B.**  

- Below ~500–550 t, low-density performance becomes heavily dependent on residual fusion propellant.
- Above ~750–800 t, additional mass yields diminishing returns relative to the dry-mass impact on the overall vehicle.
- The existing band already covers the region where residual propellant can finish deceleration without forcing the sail mass to dominate the dry-mass budget.

## 4. Residual Propellant Backup Sizing Guidance

- Size residual fusion propellant to supply the Δv shortfall corresponding to the low-density (0.05 cm⁻³) case for a 550 t sail.
- This keeps the sail mass from ballooning while preserving mission flexibility under TOLC 8.

## 5. Mass-Budget Impact

- No immediate change to the 550–750 t locked planning value is required.
- The value is now performance-linked rather than purely architectural.
- Future higher-fidelity MHD / kinetic models may tighten the band but are not expected to shift the center of gravity outside 550–750 t for the Seed Vessel.
