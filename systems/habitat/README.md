# Habitat & Artificial Gravity

## Design Approach
Multi-kilometre modular cityship using rotating structures to produce continuous artificial gravity in living zones.

### Preferred Configurations
- Counter-rotating cylinders (to cancel net angular momentum)
- Stacked Stanford-torus style rings along a non-rotating spine
- Combination of both for flexibility and expansion

## Gravity Targets
- Living and working areas: 0.8 – 1.0 g
- Industrial / research zones: variable or zero-g as needed
- Transition zones carefully designed for Coriolis comfort

## Habitat Contents
- Residential modules (private and communal)
- Parks, green spaces, and large public volumes
- Education, culture, and recreation facilities
- Medical centers and research laboratories
- Manufacturing and maintenance workshops
- Food production integrated with life support

## Psychological & Social Design
High priority is given to variety of environment, natural light simulation, community spaces, and long-term social stability. Permanent PATSAGi oversight assists with conflict resolution and mental health support under mercy principles.
