# DSA-Ref-A — Deck-by-Deck Layout Concept

**Status:** Conceptual layout baseline  
**Reference Population:** ~300  
**Spin Radius:** ~140 m  
**Owner:** Autonomicity Games Inc.  
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-11

## 1. Purpose

Translate the volume allocation into a practical multi-deck organization that supports daily life, life support, circulation, emergency response, and psychological sustainability.

## 2. Overall Organization Principles

- Gravity gradient is used intentionally: higher-g decks for living and most work; lower-g or variable zones for specialized functions where beneficial.
- Clear separation of clean living zones from technical / dirty zones.
- Multiple independent social and circulation paths to avoid single points of social or physical blockage.
- Emergency shelters and medical facilities reachable within defined time limits from any occupied deck.
- Water storage and other dual-use mass preferentially located for radiation shielding benefit.

## 3. Proposed Deck Structure (Rotating Section)

### Deck A — Primary Residential
- Private quarters for the majority of the population
- Highest priority on quiet, privacy, and restorative quality
- Local small social nodes and pantry points

### Deck B — Community & Culture
- Main dining, gathering, cultural, and recreational volumes
- Larger green / park spaces
- Primary social heart of the ship

### Deck C — Food Production & Life Support (Human-Accessible)
- Higher-plant cultivation zones with access aisles
- Food processing and preparation interfaces
- Direct visual and physical connection to community decks where practical (morale benefit)

### Deck D — Technical Life Support & Utilities
- Photobioreactors, water processing, atmosphere management
- Primary utility distribution and maintenance access
- Higher noise and technical character; separated from residential

### Deck E — Medical, Exercise & Specialized
- Clinic, isolation capability, exercise facilities
- Mental health and private consultation spaces
- Some lower-g or variable-g exercise options if architecture permits

### Additional / Partial Decks or Zones
- Workshops and light manufacturing
- Storage and contingency reserves
- Observation or visualization areas
- Transfer and airlock interfaces to the non-rotating spine

## 4. Non-Rotating Spine Functions

- Fusion propulsion, propellant tanks, magnetic nozzle
- Primary power generation and main radiators
- Docking, berthing, and external maintenance
- Zero-g research and industrial processes
- Magnetic sail stowage and deployment systems
- Command / control backups and storm shelters (high-shielding)

## 5. Circulation & Emergency

- At least two independent vertical circulation paths between major decks
- Rapid access routes to storm shelters and medical
- Ability to isolate individual decks or sectors without losing all life support or escape routes
