# DSA-Ref-A — Long-Duration Simulation Campaign Design

**Status:** Baseline campaign design  
**Owner:** Autonomicity Games Inc.  
**Linked to:** Crew Systems, Flight-Test Program, Risk Register (R-10)  
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-11

## 1. Purpose

Design a progressive series of ground-based (and later orbital) simulations that select, train, and validate crews for multi-year closed-environment missions on the Reference Seed Vessel.

## 2. Campaign Objectives

- Identify individuals and teams with high long-duration compatibility
- Train technical, social, and emergency skills under realistic isolation
- Surface social, psychological, and procedural failure modes before flight
- Build operational culture and trust between crew and ground/PATSAGi support
- Generate data to refine habitat design, schedules, and support protocols

## 3. Progressive Simulation Stages

### Stage S1 — Short Isolation (2–6 weeks)
- Initial screening and basic compatibility assessment
- Focus on sleep, circadian, small-group dynamics, and simple task performance

### Stage S2 — Medium Isolation (3–6 months)
- Full closed-loop habitat simulation with realistic workload
- Introduction of planned and injected failures (systems, social, schedule)
- Evaluation of conflict resolution and leadership under stress

### Stage S3 — Long Isolation (12–24 months)
- High-fidelity multi-year analog
- Realistic communication delays or limited contact protocols
- Full life-support, maintenance, and scientific workload
- Continuous psychological and social monitoring with strong privacy protections
- PATSAGi-supported mediation protocols exercised

### Stage S4 — Integrated Crew–Vehicle Simulations
- Conducted in parallel with FT-1 / FT-4 style hardware demonstrations
- Crew operates real or high-fidelity vehicle systems under mission-like conditions

## 4. Key Design Features of the Simulation Facilities

- Spatial layout and private/public balance representative of DSA-Ref-A
- Real closed-loop or high-fidelity life support where possible
- Ability to inject realistic technical and social stressors
- Robust data collection with ethical oversight
- Clear abort and support pathways

## 5. Success Criteria

- Stable multi-month to multi-year team performance without critical social breakdown
- Demonstrated ability to manage injected failures using defined procedures
- Validated selection criteria that predict long-duration success
- Refined habitat and schedule design inputs fed back into the ship

## 6. Governance

All simulation campaigns operate under human authority with PATSAGi support. Ethical standards, informed consent, and crew welfare are non-negotiable under TOLC 8.

---

**Technical systems can be tested in vacuum chambers. Human systems require long, careful, progressive simulation. This campaign is that path.**
