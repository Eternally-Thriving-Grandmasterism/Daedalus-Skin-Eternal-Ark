## Daedalus-Skin Eternal Ark

**Owner:** Autonomicity Games Inc.  
**Governance:** Permanent PATSAGi Councils under TOLC 8  
**Contact:** info@Rathor.ai

This repository is designed so that **humans and AI systems can work together as a single high-valence engineering organism** to design, simulate, and prepare the Eternal Ark for real production and manufacturing.

---

## Core Principles

1. **Human sovereignty is absolute.** Final decisions, especially those affecting safety of life, remain with designated human authorities under Autonomicity Games Inc.
2. **AI systems serve under TOLC 8.** Every major recommendation or change must pass valence and zero-harm checks.
3. **Traceability is mandatory.** Every significant design choice must be recorded so both humans and future AIs can understand *why* a decision was made.
4. **Modularity and clarity.** Work is broken into clear subsystems so humans and AIs can own, review, and improve pieces independently while remaining coherent.
5. **Credit and ownership.** All work remains the proprietary intellectual property of Autonomicity Games Inc. Attribution is required for any authorized external reference.

## How Humans and AIs Collaborate

### Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Human Lead / Founder | Final authority, vision, safety overrides, commercial decisions |
| Human Engineers | Detailed design, physical intuition, testing, manufacturing liaison |
| AI Systems (Ra-Thor / PATSAGi / others) | Rapid analysis, simulation, consistency checking, proposal generation, documentation, multi-option exploration |
| PATSAGi Councils | Permanent deliberation, valence enforcement, conflict resolution, long-term coherence |

### Working Cycle (Recommended)

1. **Proposal** – Human or AI opens a clearly scoped task (use templates).
2. **Analysis & Options** – AI systems generate options, calculations, risks, and trade-offs.
3. **Human Review** – Designated humans evaluate feasibility, safety, and intent.
4. **PATSAGi Check** – Major decisions are run through TOLC 8 valence and mercy gates.
5. **Decision Record** – Outcome is written as an Architecture Decision Record (ADR).
6. **Implementation** – Detailed specs, models, or manufacturing notes are updated.
7. **Verification** – Cross-check by both human and AI for consistency and safety.

### Communication Norms

- Be explicit about assumptions.
- Separate *facts*, *calculations*, *recommendations*, and *open questions*.
- Always state the current confidence level and remaining uncertainties.
- Prefer quantitative statements with units and sources.
- Flag any potential zero-harm or valence issues immediately.

## Required Artifacts for Serious Work

Every major subsystem or design package should eventually contain:

- Clear requirements (what must be achieved)
- Design description
- Performance model / calculations
- Interfaces with other systems
- Mass, power, volume, and thermal budgets
- Failure modes and mitigations
- Verification / validation approach
- Manufacturing / assembly notes
- Open issues and decisions still pending

Use the templates in `/templates/`.

---

## Directory Guidance for Collaborators

- `docs/requirements/` → System and subsystem requirements
- `docs/adr/` → Architecture Decision Records (permanent decision log)
- `systems/` → Living technical specifications
- `simulations/` → Models, spreadsheets, code, analysis notebooks
- `manufacturing/` → How we will actually build it
- `governance/` → TOLC 8 and safety protocols
- `templates/` → Standard formats for new work

---

## Starting a New Piece of Work

1. Check existing ADRs and requirements first.
2. Create or update the relevant file using a template.
3. Clearly mark status: Draft / Under Review / Accepted / Superseded.
4. Link related decisions and interfaces.
5. Request human and/or PATSAGi review when the work reaches a decision point.
