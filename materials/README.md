# Materials

Focus areas:
- Self-healing polymers, vitrimers, and hybrid biological systems (Daedalus-Skin)
- Hydrogen-rich radiation shielding materials
- High-strength, low-mass structural composites suitable for multi-century service
- Superconducting materials for magnetic nozzles and active shielding
- Thermal protection and radiator materials

All material development is owned by Autonomicity Games Inc. and aligned with Air Foundation self-healing airframe concepts where applicable.
