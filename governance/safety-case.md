# High-Level Safety Case & Critical Failure Modes

**Status:** Accepted baseline (WP-10 complete)  
**Owner:** Autonomicity Games Inc.  
**Authority:** Permanent PATSAGi Councils under TOLC 8  
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-11

## 1. Safety Philosophy

Zero-harm to human life is non-negotiable.  
The ship must remain habitable after any single major failure and must allow recovery or safe abandonment of damaged sections. Human sovereignty and override authority are preserved at all times. All critical decisions are subject to TOLC 8 valence checks.

## 2. Critical Failure Categories

### A. Propulsion & Power
- Loss of fusion drive or magnetic nozzle during burn
- Cascade failure of power reactors
- Uncontrolled thrust or attitude excursion

**Mitigations:** Multiple independent reactor clusters, rapid shutdown authority, attitude control redundancy, propellant isolation, magnetic sail as alternative deceleration path.

### B. Life Support
- Loss of primary biological loop
- Major atmospheric contamination or pressure loss
- Long-term food production failure

**Mitigations:** Dual biological + physicochemical pathways, sectional isolation, contingency stores sized for multi-month recovery, multiple independent growth and processing zones.

### C. Habitat Structure & Spin
- Bearing or seal failure in rotating section
- Major structural damage from impact
- Loss of spin (gravity)

**Mitigations:** Redundant bearings/seals where practical, rapid despin and transfer capability, independent pressure vessels, storm shelters, Daedalus-Skin first response.

### D. Radiation & Particle Environment
- Extreme solar particle event or unexpected GCR intensity
- Forward shield compromise at high speed

**Mitigations:** Multi-layer passive + active shielding, dedicated storm shelters with independent life support, continuous monitoring and early warning, trajectory and operational adjustments.

### E. Governance & Control
- Loss of primary computing / PATSAGi lattice
- Conflicting human–AI authority
- Social or psychological crisis

**Mitigations:** Multiple isolated control cores, offline sovereign modes, clear human override hierarchy, permanent council deliberation records, psychological support systems, and designed social spaces.

## 3. Core Safety Rules

1. No single failure shall cause loss of the entire crew.
2. Critical systems shall have at least triple redundancy or equivalent graceful degradation.
3. Any mode change that affects life safety requires logged decision and valence check.
4. Sectional isolation must be possible without cascading loss of life support.
5. Continuous health monitoring of structure, atmosphere, radiation, and crew.
