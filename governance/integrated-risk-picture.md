## 3. Governing Insight

The dominant risks are not exotic physics problems. They are **endurance, reliability, and closed-loop stability** problems under multi-year operation, plus the industrial capacity to build and assemble the vehicle. The technology maturation plan is correctly focused on these areas.

## 4. Immediate Program Implications

- Do not commit to full-scale orbital assembly until Threads A, B, and C show clear risk reduction.
- Trace contaminant control deserves explicit early attention.
- Mass growth in structure or shielding must be watched continuously because it cascades into propellant mass.
