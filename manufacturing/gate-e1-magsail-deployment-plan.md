## 1. Purpose

Define the first physical maturation gate (Gate E1) for the magnetic sail: subscale deployment and stability demonstration that reduces deployment and dynamics risk before larger investment.

## 2. Gate E1 Objectives

- Demonstrate reliable deployment of a representative multi-loop (MS-B style) subscale sail from a stowed configuration.
- Verify controlled attitude and basic stability under relevant dynamic conditions (vacuum preferred; atmospheric or simulated vacuum acceptable for early runs).
- Collect data on deployment kinematics, residual oscillations, and control authority.
- Validate key interfaces and mechanisms that appear in the spine ICD.

## 3. Success Criteria (Minimum)

| Criterion | Threshold |
|-----------|-----------|
| Deployment success rate | ≥ 90 % across a defined test series |
| Time to stable deployed configuration | Within design target (to be set for the subscale) |
| Residual dynamics | Damped to acceptable levels without structural damage |
| Control authority | Demonstrated ability to trim attitude after deployment |
| No unrecoverable single-point failures in the test article | Observed |

## 4. Test Article Guidance

- Scale: large enough to exercise multi-loop current sharing and staged release; small enough for ground or suborbital test.
- Conductor: representative HTS or advanced conductor segments (full flight current not required for early Gate E1).
- Structure: representative of the chosen MS-B packaging approach.
- Instrumentation: high-rate kinematics, current, temperature, and structural strain.

## 5. Test Environments (Progressive)

1. Ground ambient / vacuum chamber deployment kinematics
2. Thermal-vacuum deployment and stability
3. Optional suborbital or orbital free-flight demonstration if ground results are successful

## 6. Link to Higher Gates

- Gate E1 success is a prerequisite for larger-scale deployment articles and for confidence in the residual-fusion handoff logic under sail-health criteria.
- Gate E2 will address plasma interaction / drag validation.
- Gate E3 will be the flight-relevant demonstration (FT-3 class).

## 7. Governance

Gate E1 results are reviewed by PATSAGi under TOLC 8 before any decision to scale the sail system or to relax residual-propellant margins.
