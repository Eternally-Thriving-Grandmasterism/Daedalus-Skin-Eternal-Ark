# Progressive Flight-Test & Demonstration Program — DSA-Ref-A

**Status:** Baseline program definition  
**Owner:** Autonomicity Games Inc.  
**Linked to:** Technology Maturation Plan, Integrated Risk Picture, Development Roadmap  
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-11

## 1. Purpose

Define a progressive series of flight demonstrations that systematically retire the highest risks before the full Reference Seed Vessel is committed to a multi-year interstellar-capable mission.

## 2. Program Logic

Risk is retired in layers:

1. Ground and orbital technology demonstrations (already in Technology Maturation Plan)
2. Subscale or partial-system flight tests
3. Integrated but limited-duration Seed Vessel shakedown missions
4. Full-duration demonstration mission

No layer is skipped while its dominant risks remain high.

## 3. Proposed Flight-Test Sequence

### FT-1 — Orbital Spin & Life Support Demonstrator
- Rotating habitat module (reduced radius or length) with closed-loop life support
- Demonstrate multi-month rotation, seal performance, utility transfer, and closed-loop stability in true space conditions
- Primary risk retirement: Habitat mechanics (H-01 to H-05) and early life support (LS-01 to LS-05)

### FT-2 — Propulsion & Power Endurance Flight
- Non-crewed or minimally crewed free-flying platform with fusion drive and magnetic nozzle
- Multi-month pulsed or continuous operation in deep space or high orbit
- Primary risk retirement: Fusion sustainment and nozzle life (P-01, P-02)

### FT-3 — Magnetic Sail Deployment & Drag Demonstration
- Deployable superconducting sail on a dedicated or shared platform
- Measure drag performance against real solar wind / interplanetary plasma as a proxy
- Primary risk retirement: Sail deployment, stability, and performance uncertainty

### FT-4 — Integrated DSA-Ref-A Shakedown
- Full Reference Seed Vessel in cislunar or high solar orbit
- Progressive spin-up, life support closure, limited propulsion tests, sail deployment rehearsal
- Duration: months to ~2 years
- Primary goal: System-level integration and operational learning

### FT-5 — Full Demonstration Mission
- Multi-year mission exercising the complete architecture at the design cruise capability
- Continuous data return to refine subsequent vessels

## 4. Governance Gates

Each flight-test stage requires:
- Updated FMEA and risk register
- PATSAGi / TOLC 8 review
- Human authority go/no-go decision
- Clear success criteria before the next stage is funded at full scale
