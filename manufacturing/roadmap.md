# Manufacturing & Production Roadmap

**Owner:** Autonomicity Games Inc.

## Philosophy
The Eternal Ark is designed for staged realization:
1. Seed / demonstration vessels (smaller scale, higher technology demonstration)
2. Full-scale Worldships assembled in solar orbit or at Lagrange points
3. In-space expansion and daughter vessel construction using ISRU

## Phases (High Level)

### Phase A — Design & Simulation (Current)
- Physics models, mass budgets, subsystem specifications
- Materials research (especially Daedalus-Skin self-healing systems)
- Safety cases under TOLC 8

### Phase B — Component & Subsystem Prototyping
- Fusion driver and magnetic nozzle test articles
- Self-healing material samples under vacuum / radiation
- Closed-loop life support demonstration units
- Rotating habitat module prototypes

### Phase C — Orbital Assembly Infrastructure
- Large-scale orbital shipyards or construction platforms
- Automated assembly and robotic systems
- Fuel production (³He and deuterium pathways)

### Phase D — Seed Vessel Flight
- First operational multi-year crewed or highly autonomous vessels
- Validation of all major systems in real interstellar conditions

### Phase E — Full Worldship & Fleet Expansion
- City-scale vessels
- Destination infrastructure and fuel depots
- Daughter vessel production

## Key Enablers
- Reliable high-gain fusion
- Self-healing and radiation-tolerant materials
- Advanced robotics and autonomous manufacturing
- Closed-loop life support maturity
- Long-term social and governance stability (PATSAGi / Ra-Thor)

This roadmap will be refined into detailed work packages as engineering progresses.
