# Technology Maturation Milestones & Success Metrics

**Status:** Detailed schedule baseline  
**Owner:** Autonomicity Games Inc.  
**Linked to:** Technology Maturation Plan, Integrated Risk Picture, Flight-Test Program  
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-11

## 1. Purpose

Translate the technology maturation threads into concrete milestones with measurable success criteria that gate further investment and flight-test progression.

## 2. Milestone Structure

Each major thread has three progressive gates:

- **Gate 1 — Component / Subscale Proof**
- **Gate 2 — Integrated System Demonstration**
- **Gate 3 — Endurance / Flight-Relevant Validation**

No full-scale Seed Vessel assembly commitment occurs until the highest-risk threads reach Gate 2 or better.

---

## 3. Thread Milestones

### Thread A — Fusion Drive & Magnetic Nozzle

| Gate | Milestone | Success Metrics |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| A1 | High-rep-rate driver + single-nozzle test article | Stable pulsed operation for >100 continuous hours at relevant power; thermal margins demonstrated |
| A2 | Multi-month endurance run | >1,000 hours cumulative operation with acceptable erosion and performance retention |
| A3 | Flight-relevant demonstration (FT-2) | Multi-month operation in space environment with telemetry matching ground predictions |

### Thread B — Rotating Habitat Mechanics

| Gate | Milestone | Success Metrics |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| B1 | Vacuum bearing + seal test article | Continuous rotation >6 months with acceptable leak and wear rates |
| B2 | Utility transfer + balance demonstration | Dual-path power/data/fluid transfer under rotation; active balance control validated |
| B3 | Orbital spin demonstrator (FT-1) | Multi-month crewed or high-fidelity simulated habitat rotation in space |

### Thread C — Closed-Loop Life Support

| Gate | Milestone | Success Metrics |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| C1 | Integrated ground testbed | Stable closed-loop atmosphere + water for >6 months with human metabolic simulator |
| C2 | Multi-year ground or orbital loop | >18–24 months continuous operation with contingency recovery demonstrated |
| C3 | FT-1 / FT-4 integrated demonstration | Closed-loop performance in true space conditions at relevant scale |

### Thread D — Daedalus-Skin

| Gate | Milestone | Success Metrics |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| D1 | Vacuum + radiation sample testing | Healing function retained after relevant fluence and thermal cycling |
| D2 | Impact and puncture demonstration | Autonomous sealing of defined defect sizes under vacuum |
| D3 | Long-duration exposure article | Multi-year aging data supporting multi-decade service life claim |

### Thread E — Magnetic Sail

| Gate | Milestone | Success Metrics |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| E1 | Subscale deployment + stability | Reliable deployment and controlled attitude in relevant environment |
| E2 | Plasma interaction validation | Drag measurements matching models within acceptable error bands |
| E3 | FT-3 flight demonstration | In-space deployment and measurable drag against solar wind / plasma |

### Thread F — Radiation Shielding

| Gate | Milestone | Success Metrics |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| F1 | Dose model validation | Models agree with available flight and ground data within defined tolerance |
| F2 | Active vs passive trade closure | Quantitative mass + power comparison decides baseline approach |
| F3 | Storm shelter and dual-use water verification | Integrated shielding performance confirmed in design reviews |

## 4. Program Gate Rule

- **Full DSA-Ref-A assembly authorization** requires Threads A, B, and C at Gate 2 minimum, with clear path to Gate 3.
- PATSAGi / TOLC 8 review and human authority sign-off are mandatory at each major gate.
