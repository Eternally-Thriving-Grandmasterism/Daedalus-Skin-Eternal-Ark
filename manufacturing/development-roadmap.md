# Development & Assembly Roadmap — To Reference Seed Vessel and Beyond

**Status:** Baseline (WP-14 complete)  
**Owner:** Autonomicity Games Inc.  
**Target:** DSA-Ref-A Reference Seed Vessel  
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-11

## Phase 0 — Architecture (Complete)
- Requirements, ADRs, performance models, interfaces, safety case, fuel strategy, Reference Seed definition and preliminary mass budget.

## Phase 1 — Detailed Design & Technology Maturation (Current Next Phase)
- Close the major quantitative uncertainties in the mass budget (structure, shielding, sail, life support, tank fractions).
- Develop and test critical technology elements on the ground and in Earth orbit:
  - High-rep-rate ICF driver and magnetic nozzle test articles
  - Self-healing Daedalus-Skin samples under vacuum and radiation
  - Closed-loop life support demonstration units at relevant scale
  - Superconducting magnetic sail subscale deployments
  - Rotating habitat bearing/seal and transfer mechanism prototypes
- Produce a refined, traceable mass and power budget for DSA-Ref-A.

## Phase 2 — Orbital Infrastructure & Component Production
- Establish or gain access to large-scale orbital construction capability (cislunar or solar orbit shipyard).
- Begin production of major structural elements, tanks, radiators, and habitat modules.
- Develop automated and robotic assembly systems.
- Initiate lunar and/or early gas-giant ³He acquisition demonstrations.

## Phase 3 — Assembly of the Reference Seed Vessel
- Modular assembly of DSA-Ref-A in orbit.
- Progressive integration and integrated system testing (power, life support, spin-up, propulsion cold-flow / limited hot-fire as feasible).
- Crew training and long-duration simulation campaigns synchronized with the physical ship.

## Phase 4 — Flight Demonstration
- First operational mission of the Seed Vessel (initially limited duration, then extending toward the 10–25 year design goal).
- Continuous data return to refine the design of subsequent vessels.
- Validation of the full architecture under real conditions.

## Phase 5 — Scale-Up to Worldships
- Apply lessons from the Seed Vessel to the first full Patsagi-Mercy class Worldships.
- Expand fuel infrastructure and shipyard capacity.
- Begin multi-ship fleet operations and eventual interstellar expansion.

---

**Governing principle:** Each phase must reduce the dominant risks identified in the risk register before major resources are committed to the next phase. Human sovereignty and TOLC 8 valence checks remain in force throughout.
