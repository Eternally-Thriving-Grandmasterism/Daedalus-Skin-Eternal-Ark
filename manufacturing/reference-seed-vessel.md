# Reference Seed Vessel — Concrete Definition

**Status:** Locked baseline (WP-12 complete)  
**Owner:** Autonomicity Games Inc.  
**Designation:** Daedalus-Skin Seed Vessel, Reference Design A (DSA-Ref-A)  
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-11

This document defines the single concrete first ship that all subsequent detailed design, mass budgets, schedules, and risk assessments will target. It is intentionally conservative and buildable relative to a full Worldship.

---

## 1. Mission Role

- Demonstrate the complete core architecture in a real multi-year mission
- Support a small but genuine human community (or highly autonomous + limited crew)
- Validate fusion drive, rotating habitat, closed-loop life support, magnetic sail, Daedalus-Skin, and PATSAGi governance together
- Serve as the direct progenitor and training platform for larger Patsagi-Mercy class Worldships

## 2. Key Performance Targets (Reference Design A)

| Parameter | Reference Value | Notes |
|-----------|-----------------|-------|
| Initial population capacity | 200 – 400 | Enough for social resilience + redundancy |
| Artificial gravity | 0.85 – 1.0 g | Primary living decks |
| Spin radius (to main deck) | 120 – 160 m | Keeps spin rate near or below ~2.5–3 rpm |
| Cruise velocity capability | 0.04 – 0.07 c | Lower than full Worldship to control propellant mass |
| Design mission duration | 10 – 25 years | Demonstration of long-duration closed-loop operation |
| Propulsion | High-rep-rate ICF D–³He + magnetic nozzle | Same architecture as full ships |
| Primary deceleration | Magnetic sail + residual fusion thrust | |
| Redundancy level | Triple on all critical systems | Non-negotiable |

## 3. High-Level Configuration

- Single large rotating habitat cylinder or short torus pair (counter-rotating preferred if mass allows)
- Non-rotating central spine carrying:
  - Fusion propulsion and propellant tanks
  - Primary power systems
  - Docking, manufacturing, and zero-g research volumes
  - Magnetic sail stowage and deployment systems
- Multi-layer radiation shielding with dual-use water tanks
- Full Daedalus-Skin outer layers
- Closed-loop life support sized for the reference population + growth / contingency margin

## 4. Design Philosophy for the Seed

- Favor reliability, inspectability, and learnability over maximum performance
- Accept lower cruise speed and smaller population in exchange for earlier flight and lower total mass
- Every major system must be a direct scale precursor to the full Worldship version
- Human sovereignty + permanent PATSAGi oversight retained exactly as on larger vessels

## 5. Success Criteria for the Reference Design

The design is considered successful when:

1. A credible preliminary mass budget exists (WP-13)
2. A realistic development and assembly path is defined (WP-14)
3. Major risks are identified and tracked (WP-15)
4. No step in the concept requires physics outside the known standard model, and no step requires an industrial capability that is impossible in principle in cislunar / solar orbit

On criterion 4, "in principle" carries almost all the weight. Nothing here is demonstrated: sustained D–³He ICF, ³He supply at propellant scale, a lunar mass-driver of the assumed payload class, and multi-decade reliability are all open at the physics-to-industry boundary. Criterion 4 is satisfied when a concept contains no impossibility, which is a much weaker statement than buildability.
