# Technology Maturation & Test Plan — DSA-Ref-A

**Status:** Baseline plan  
**Owner:** Autonomicity Games Inc.  
**Linked to:** Development Roadmap & Risk Register  
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-11

## 1. Purpose

Systematically reduce the highest risks identified in the risk register and FMEA before committing to full Seed Vessel construction.

## 2. Priority Technology Threads

### Thread A — Fusion Drive & Magnetic Nozzle
- Ground-based high-repetition-rate driver and pellet injection tests
- Magnetic nozzle thermal and endurance testing under representative heat loads
- Long-duration pulsed operation demonstrations
- Target: Raise confidence that multi-year burn is achievable

### Thread B — Rotating Habitat Mechanics
- Subscale spin-bearing and seal test articles in vacuum
- Utility transfer (power, data, fluids) across rotating interface
- Dynamic balancing and fault recovery demonstrations
- Target: Prove multi-year continuous rotation with maintainable seals

### Thread C — Closed-Loop Life Support
- Integrated photobioreactor + higher-plant + physicochemical testbeds
- Multi-month to multi-year closed-loop runs with human metabolic simulators
- Contamination and recovery scenario testing
- Target: Demonstrate stable operation at relevant scale

### Thread D — Daedalus-Skin & Materials
- Vacuum + radiation exposure of candidate self-healing systems
- Impact and puncture healing demonstrations
- Long-term aging characterization
- Target: Qualify outer skin for multi-decade service

### Thread E — Magnetic Sail
- Subscale superconducting loop deployment and stability tests
- Plasma interaction experiments / high-fidelity simulation validation
- Structural and thermal design of large lightweight conductors
- Target: Reduce performance and deployment uncertainty

### Thread F — Radiation Shielding
- Dose modeling validation and material characterization
- Active magnetic shielding subscale demonstrations (if trade remains positive)
- Target: Close the passive vs. active mass trade with data

## 3. Integration with Roadmap

- Phase 1 (Detailed Design & Technology Maturation) is dominated by these threads.
- No major orbital assembly commitment until the highest RPN items (especially P-01, P-02, life support stability, and spin mechanics) show clear risk reduction.
- PATSAGi / TOLC 8 reviews are required at the end of each major test campaign before proceeding.

## 4. Success Metrics

Each thread must produce:
- Quantified performance data
- Updated FMEA scores
- Updated mass / power / reliability estimates
- Clear go / no-go recommendation for the next program phase
