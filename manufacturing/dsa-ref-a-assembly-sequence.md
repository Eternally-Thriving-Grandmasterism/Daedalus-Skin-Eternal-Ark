# DSA-Ref-A — Orbital Assembly Sequence

**Status:** Detailed planning baseline  
**Reference Design:** DSA-Ref-A  
**Owner:** Autonomicity Games Inc.  
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-11

## 1. Overall Strategy

The Reference Seed Vessel is assembled in cislunar or high solar orbit using modular elements launched or manufactured in space. Assembly prioritizes early creation of a pressurized, powered, and rotatable core so that subsequent work can be performed in a shirt-sleeve or controlled environment wherever possible.

## 2. High-Level Sequence

### Stage A — Spine and Propulsion Core
1. Deliver and assemble the non-rotating central spine
2. Integrate primary power reactors, radiators, and distribution
3. Install propulsion engines, magnetic nozzle, and main propellant tank interfaces
4. Verify power, propulsion, and basic attitude control

### Stage B — Rotating Habitat Primary Structure
1. Assemble or deploy the primary rotating pressure vessel / cylinder
2. Install spin bearings, seals, and transfer mechanisms onto the spine
3. Connect utilities (power, data, fluids) across the rotating interface
4. Perform initial spin tests at low rate

### Stage C — Habitat Outfitting & Life Support
1. Install decks, partitions, and primary life support equipment
2. Load water inventory (dual-use shielding)
3. Install photobioreactors, plant growth systems, and physicochemical backups
4. Close the atmosphere and begin closed-loop verification

### Stage D — Shielding, Skin, and External Systems
1. Complete passive shielding placement
2. Apply Daedalus-Skin outer layers
3. Install forward particle shield
4. Integrate and stow magnetic sail system

### Stage E — Final Integration & Verification
1. Full system integrated testing (power, life support, spin, propulsion cold-flow)
2. Load remaining propellant (or propellant transfer interfaces)
3. Crew training campaigns synchronized with the physical vehicle
4. Final safety reviews under TOLC 8 and human authority

## 3. Key Enabling Capabilities Required

- Large-scale orbital construction and robotic assembly
- High-capacity power and thermal rejection during assembly
- Reliable rotating seals and utility transfer systems
- In-space manufacturing or efficient launch of large structural elements
- Rigorous configuration control and quality assurance

## 4. Risk Controls During Assembly

- Never rely on a single untested critical path for life support or structural integrity
- Maintain abort-to-safe-haven options at every major stage
- Continuous PATSAGi monitoring of assembly decisions and anomalies
