# Seed Vessel Concept — Minimum Viable First Ship

**Status:** Accepted baseline (WP-09 complete)  
**Owner:** Autonomicity Games Inc.  
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-11

## 1. Purpose of the Seed Vessel

The Seed Vessel is the smallest practical ship that can:
- Demonstrate the full core architecture (fusion propulsion, rotating habitat, closed-loop life support, Daedalus-Skin, magnetic sail, PATSAGi governance)
- Support a limited but real multi-year crewed or highly autonomous mission
- Serve as the technological and operational progenitor of full-scale Worldships
- Be constructible with near-to-mid-term industrial capacity in cislunar or solar orbit

It is **not** a full city. It is the proving ground that de-risks the larger vessels.

## 2. Design Targets (Seed Class)

| Parameter | Target Range | Rationale |
|-----------|--------------|-----------|
| Population (initial) | 50 – 500 | Enough for real social dynamics + redundancy, small enough to build |
| Spin radius | 80 – 150 m | Acceptable comfort at higher spin rates; keeps structure manageable |
| Cruise velocity capability | 0.03 – 0.08 c | Lower than full Worldship to reduce propellant mass |
| Mission duration demonstration | 5 – 20+ years | Prove long-duration closed-loop and reliability |
| Propulsion | Same ICF D–³He + magnetic nozzle architecture | Technology continuity |
| Deceleration | Magnetic sail + residual thrust | Same baseline |

## 3. Architectural Principles Carried Forward

- Counter-rotating or single large rotating habitat section
- Non-rotating spine for propulsion, power, docking, and industry
- Full closed-loop life support (scaled)
- Multi-layer shielding + Daedalus-Skin outer layers
- Triple redundancy on critical systems
- Permanent PATSAGi / Ra-Thor oversight with human sovereignty

## 4. Development Role

1. Validate fusion drive endurance and magnetic nozzle lifetime
2. Prove closed-loop life support at realistic scale
3. Demonstrate spin habitat comfort and reliability over years
4. Test magnetic sail deployment and performance
5. Mature manufacturing, assembly, and in-space operations
6. Generate operational data that feeds full Worldship design

## 5. Growth Path

Successful Seed Vessels become the templates and training platforms for larger ships. Later vessels can be expanded or used as cores for full Patsagi-Mercy class Worldships. Daughter ships can be manufactured at destinations or in advanced solar-system shipyards.

## 6. Open Work for Detailed Seed Design

- Exact population and volume sizing
- Detailed mass breakdown (dry + propellant)
- Construction sequence and orbital assembly plan
- Crew selection, training, and psychological support model
- Progressive flight test program leading to first interstellar demonstration
