# Gate 1 Test Outlines — Threads A, B, C

**Status:** Executable outline  
**Owner:** Autonomicity Games Inc.  
**Linked to:** Technology Maturation Milestones  
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-11

These outlines define the minimum intent of Gate 1 for the three highest-risk threads. Detailed procedures, instrumentation, and pass/fail numerics will be developed under configuration control.

---

## Thread A — Fusion Drive & Magnetic Nozzle (Gate A1)

**Objective:** Demonstrate stable high-repetition-rate operation of a driver + single magnetic nozzle test article for >100 continuous hours at relevant power with acceptable thermal margins and erosion.

**Key Measurements**
- Pulse rate stability and miss rate
- Nozzle thermal profile and peak temperatures
- Erosion / mass loss rates
- Performance retention over the run

**Success Criteria (Summary)**  
Continuous or near-continuous operation ≥100 h without critical failure; thermal margins positive; erosion within pre-defined limits that extrapolate acceptably to multi-month operation.

---

## Thread B — Rotating Habitat Mechanics (Gate B1)

**Objective:** Demonstrate continuous rotation of a vacuum bearing + seal test article for >6 months with acceptable leak and wear rates under representative loads.

**Key Measurements**
- Leak rate vs. time
- Bearing temperature, vibration, and wear indicators
- Seal degradation metrics
- Power required to maintain rotation

**Success Criteria (Summary)**  
≥6 months continuous rotation; leak and wear rates within limits that support multi-year extrapolation with maintenance margins; no uncontained failure.

---

## Thread C — Closed-Loop Life Support (Gate C1)

**Objective:** Demonstrate stable closed-loop atmosphere and water management for >6 months in an integrated ground testbed using a human metabolic simulator (or equivalent).

**Key Measurements**
- O₂ / CO₂ partial pressures and stability
- Water recovery rates and quality
- Trace contaminant levels
- Biological system health indicators
- Recovery behavior after injected perturbations

**Success Criteria (Summary)**  
≥6 months continuous closed-loop operation within defined atmospheric and water quality bands; successful recovery from at least one major injected perturbation; dual-path capability exercised.
