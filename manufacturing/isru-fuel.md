# ³He and Deuterium Acquisition & ISRU Pathways

**Status:** Accepted baseline (WP-11 complete)  
**Owner:** Autonomicity Games Inc.  
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-11

## 1. The Fuel Problem

D–³He inertial confinement fusion requires significant quantities of helium-3. Terrestrial ³He is extremely scarce. Any realistic interstellar architecture must solve acquisition at scale.

## 2. Primary Acquisition Pathways

### A. Lunar Regolith
- ³He is implanted in lunar regolith by the solar wind.
- Extraction is energy-intensive but operationally closer than gas-giant mining for early campaigns.
- Suitable for initial Seed Vessel and early fleet propellant loads.

### B. Gas Giant Atmospheres (Jupiter, Saturn, etc.)
- Far higher total ³He inventory.
- Requires atmospheric mining vehicles (aerostats, skimmers, or cryogenic collectors) and substantial infrastructure.
- Long-term primary source for large Worldship fleets.

### C. Solar Wind / Space-Based Collection
- Direct collection of solar-wind ³He using large electromagnetic or material collectors in deep space or at Lagrange points.
- Lower density but continuous and infrastructure-light once established.

### D. Deuterium
- Abundant in terrestrial oceans and in icy bodies throughout the solar system.
- Not considered a limiting resource compared with ³He.

## 3. ISRU Strategy for the Eternal Ark Program

1. **Near-term (Seed Vessels):** Lunar ³He + terrestrial/near-Earth deuterium. Accept higher cost for lower logistical complexity.
2. **Mid-term:** Establish gas-giant atmospheric mining infrastructure. Begin stockpiling for full Worldships.
3. **Long-term / Destination:** Every major ship carries the knowledge and seed equipment to bootstrap local ISRU (lunar-style regolith, icy moons, or stellar-wind collection) so that daughter vessels can be fueled without returning to Sol.

## 4. Design Implications for the Ships

- Propellant tanks and loading interfaces must support multiple source chemistries and loading methods.
- Ships should carry ISRU demonstration or bootstrap packages when feasible.
- Fuel logistics become a major program-level industry, not an afterthought.
