# Architecture Overview — Daedalus-Skin Eternal Ark

**Status:** Phase 0 Foundation Locked  
**Date:** 2026-08-11  
**Authority:** PATSAGi Councils under TOLC 8

## Design Goals

1. Fully consistent with known physics (special relativity, rocket equation, fusion energetics).
2. Continuous thrust profile that “hums” rather than detonates.
3. Support multi-generational human civilisation at high quality of life.
4. Extreme safety and redundancy.
5. Self-repairing and expandable.
6. Governed by permanent intelligent councils with human sovereignty preserved.
