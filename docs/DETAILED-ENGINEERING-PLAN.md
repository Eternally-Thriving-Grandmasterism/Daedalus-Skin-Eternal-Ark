# Detailed Engineering & Testing Phase — Recommended Near-Term Work Plan

**Status:** Phase entry plan  
**Owner:** Autonomicity Games Inc.  
**Authority:** PATSAGi Councils under TOLC 8  
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-11

## 1. Purpose

Provide a clear, prioritized set of work packages for the first period of the Detailed Engineering, Simulation & Technology Maturation Phase.

## 2. Priority Order (First 12–24 Months Focus)

### P1 — Close the Largest Mass & Performance Uncertainties
1. Material selection and qualified allowable definition for the rotating habitat
2. Global finite-element model of the 140 m class habitat under pressure + spin + deck loads
3. Updated rotating-habitat structural mass number → revise reconciled mass budget
4. Quantitative passive shielding dose calculations and water-placement optimization
5. Active vs. passive shielding trade closure with numbers

### P2 — Retire Highest Technical Risks via Maturation Gates
1. Thread A (Fusion / Nozzle) Gate 1 execution
2. Thread B (Spin Mechanics) Gate 1 execution
3. Thread C (Life Support) Gate 1 execution
4. Trace contaminant long-duration studies (high FMEA RPN)

### P3 — Sail & Deceleration Confidence
1. Higher-fidelity magnetic sail drag modeling across realistic density ranges
2. Subscale deployment and stability demonstrations (Thread E Gate 1)
3. Residual propellant budget sensitivity to sail under-performance

### P4 — Human Systems & Operations
1. Detailed equipment-level layout from the deck concept
2. Begin Stage S1–S2 simulation campaign design and facility requirements
3. Crew selection criteria refinement

### P5 — Program & Industrial
1. Industrial capacity gap analysis against the requirements in industrial-capacity-analysis.md
2. Parallel fuel-acquisition pathway studies (lunar ³He priority for Seed)
3. Configuration management and data package standards for the phase

## 3. Governing Gates

- No full-scale DSA-Ref-A assembly authorization until Threads A, B, and C reach Gate 2 minimum.
- Every major numerical change >15% to a mass category updates the reconciled mass budget and risk picture.
- PATSAGi / TOLC 8 review + human authority sign-off at each major gate.

## 4. Success Definition for Early Phase

The early Detailed Engineering phase is successful when:

- Structural mass is narrowed by FE-confirmed, material-specific numbers
- Shielding mass is based on calculated dose rather than broad ranges
- At least Gate 1 is passed (or clearly on track) for Threads A, B, and C
- Sail performance uncertainty is quantified rather than open-ended
- The mass budget and risk picture remain living and current
