## 5. Formal Recommendation to the Councils

2. **Accept the optimised dual-use water + 450–750 t dedicated shielding** as the baseline radiation protection approach.
3. **Authorise an immediate update** to `manufacturing/reference-seed-mass-budget.md` incorporating these Cycle-01 numbers.
4. Retain Al-Li and hybrid paths as documented alternatives until higher-fidelity FEA and transport confirm the ranking.
