# System Requirements — Daedalus-Skin Eternal Ark

**Status:** Draft v0.1  
**Owner:** Autonomicity Games Inc.  
**Authority:** PATSAGi Councils under TOLC 8

## 1. Mission Statement

Enable continuous, multi-generational human presence across interstellar distances using only known physics, continuous fusion propulsion, closed-loop life support, self-healing structures, and permanent intelligent governance, while maintaining high quality of life and extreme safety.

## 2. Primary Requirements

### SR-1 Propulsion
- Shall provide continuous or near-continuous thrust using D–³He inertial confinement fusion with magnetic nozzle.
- Shall be capable of achieving cruise velocities in the range 0.05–0.12 c with practical mass ratios via staging.
- Shall support low-acceleration boost profiles compatible with long-duration human habitation.

### SR-2 Habitat
- Shall provide artificial gravity of 0.8–1.0 g in primary living areas via rotation.
- Shall support an initial population of at least 10,000 with growth path to 100,000+.
- Shall include full urban functions: housing, food production, medical, education, culture, industry, and recreation.

### SR-3 Life Support
- Shall operate as a near-closed-loop regenerative system for multi-decade to multi-century missions.
- Shall recover >98% of water and process metabolic waste into usable resources.
- Shall produce sufficient food and oxygen for the design population plus growth margin.

### SR-4 Structure & Protection
- Shall incorporate multi-layer radiation and particle shielding sufficient for long-duration interstellar cruise.
- Shall include self-healing outer layers ("Daedalus-Skin").
- Shall be modular and expandable in flight or at destination.

### SR-5 Safety & Reliability
- Shall implement triple (or higher) redundancy on all critical systems.
- Shall maintain habitability after any single major failure.
- Shall include storm shelters and rapid isolation capability.

### SR-6 Governance & Control
- Shall operate under permanent PATSAGi Councils with TOLC 8 valence enforcement.
- Shall preserve human sovereignty and override authority.
- Shall maintain full auditability of major decisions.

### SR-7 Manufacturing & Expansion
- Shall be designed for staged construction (seed vessel → full Worldship).
- Shall support in-space assembly and later ISRU-based expansion and daughter vessel production.

## 3. Constraints

- No faster-than-light propulsion or exotic matter.
- Must remain consistent with special relativity and the rocket equation.
- All work remains proprietary intellectual property of Autonomicity Games Inc.

## 4. Verification Approach (High Level)

- Analysis and simulation
- Subsystem prototyping
- Integrated ground and orbital testing of critical elements
- Progressive flight demonstration via seed vessels
