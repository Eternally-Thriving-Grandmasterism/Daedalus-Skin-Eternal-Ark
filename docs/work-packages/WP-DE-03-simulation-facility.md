## 1. Objective

Define the physical and operational requirements for the ground-based simulation facilities that will execute Stages S1 and S2 of the DSA-Ref-A long-duration simulation campaign. Early definition prevents facility design from lagging behind technical maturation.

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 Stage S1 Facility (2–6 weeks isolation)
- Capacity: multiple parallel small-crew modules (4–8 persons each) or one larger module
- Spatial layout: private quarters + shared work / social volume representative of DSA-Ref-A proportions
- Life support: high-fidelity or real closed-loop atmosphere and water preferred; high-quality simulation acceptable for S1
- Monitoring: continuous environmental, physiological, and selected behavioural data with strong privacy protections
- Stress injection: controlled technical and schedule stressors
- Support: rapid abort capability and psychological support access

### 2.2 Stage S2 Facility (3–6 months isolation)
- Higher fidelity closed-loop life support (or real systems)
- Full realistic workload (maintenance, science, operations, food production analogs)
- Ability to inject multi-day cascading failures
- Communication delay or limited-contact protocols
- Robust ethical oversight and continuous crew-welfare monitoring
- Data architecture that feeds design feedback into habitat and schedule refinement

### 2.3 Common Requirements Across Stages
- Spatial and volumetric similarity to the reference deck layout (private/public balance, circulation, noise isolation)
- Ability to reconfigure internal layout between campaigns
- Integration with PATSAGi monitoring and mediation protocols
- Clear configuration control of the facility itself so that results remain comparable across campaigns

## 3. Deliverables

1. Facility Requirements Document (FRD) for S1 and S2
2. Preliminary facility concept(s) and siting considerations
3. Rough order-of-magnitude cost and schedule envelope
4. Ethical and crew-welfare framework aligned with TOLC 8
5. Data collection and feedback loop definition into ship design

## 4. Success Criteria

- Facility requirements are complete enough to begin architectural and engineering design of the physical analog(s)
- Ethical framework is accepted by human authority and PATSAGi
- Clear path exists from S1/S2 results back into habitat design and crew selection criteria

## 5. Governance

Crew welfare, informed consent, and the right to abort are non-negotiable under TOLC 8. All simulation design decisions that affect human subjects require explicit human authority sign-off in addition to PATSAGi deliberation.
