## 1. Objective

Produce higher-fidelity drag models under reference interstellar flux, define packaging interface control documents with the non-rotating spine, and update mass sensitivity so that the 550–750 t MS-B planning value can be confirmed or refined.

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 Higher-Fidelity Drag Models
- Drag force / deceleration rate vs. magnetic moment across a realistic range of interstellar plasma densities
- Sensitivity to sail orientation, plasma velocity, and magnetic field topology (single vs multi-loop)
- Deceleration time estimates for DSA-Ref-A from 0.055 c under reference and low-density cases
- Identification of minimum magnetic moment required to keep deceleration within the target window (years to low tens of years)

### 2.2 Packaging Interface Control
- Stowage volume, mass properties, and attachment interfaces with the non-rotating central spine
- Deployment sequence, clearances, and keep-out zones relative to forward particle shield and residual nozzle plume
- Thermal, electrical, and data interfaces for cryogenics, current leads, and attitude control
- Failure modes and recovery paths that affect spine structural design

### 2.3 Mass-Sensitivity Update
- How total sail mass scales with required magnetic moment (and therefore with deceleration performance)
- Impact of density variation on the required moment and resulting mass
- Updated recommendation for the mass-budget planning value if higher-fidelity results shift the 550–750 t band

## 3. Deliverables

1. Refined drag coefficient / deceleration tables under reference flux (this cycle)
2. Interface Control Document (ICD) between magnetic sail and non-rotating spine
3. Mass-sensitivity curves and updated planning recommendation
4. Risk register updates for performance shortfall and deployment failure

## 4. Success Criteria

- Drag performance is expressed as quantitative functions of magnetic moment and plasma density
- Packaging interfaces are defined at a level sufficient for spine structural design
- Mass planning value is either confirmed or explicitly revised with traceable rationale
- Residual fusion propellant backup remains explicitly sized against the residual performance risk

## 5. Governance

No final performance claim is locked until higher-fidelity plasma models and Gate E1 data exist. Residual fusion propellant remains non-negotiable under TOLC 8.
