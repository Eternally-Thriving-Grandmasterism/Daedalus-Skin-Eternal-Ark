# ADR-0002: Use of Rotating Structures for Artificial Gravity

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-08-11  
**Deciders:** PATSAGi Councils + Human Lead

## Context

Long-duration exposure to microgravity causes significant physiological harm. For multi-generational Worldships we require continuous artificial gravity in primary living areas at levels close to Earth normal.

## Decision

Primary living and working habitats shall use rotation (counter-rotating cylinders and/or stacked tori) to generate 0.8–1.0 g artificial gravity. A non-rotating spine will carry propulsion, industrial, and zero-g research functions.

## Consequences

**Positive**
- Proven physics, no exotic technology required
- Continuous gravity without constant thrust
- Psychological and physiological benefits for long-term crews

**Negative / Risks**
- Coriolis effects must be managed (radius and spin rate trade-off)
- Structural complexity of rotating seals and bearings
- Docking and transfer between rotating and non-rotating sections

## Alternatives Considered

- Continuous 1 g thrust — rejected due to enormous propellant cost
- Centrifuge modules only for exercise — insufficient for multi-generational health
- Pharmacological / genetic countermeasures alone — not accepted as primary solution

## Follow-up Work

- Spin radius vs. comfort analysis
- Bearing and seal design concepts
- Transfer mechanism between rotating and non-rotating volumes
