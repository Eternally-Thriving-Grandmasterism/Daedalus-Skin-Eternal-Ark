# ADR-0001: Primary Propulsion — High-Repetition-Rate ICF with D–³He and Magnetic Nozzle

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-08-11  
**Deciders:** PATSAGi Councils + Human Lead (Autonomicity Games Inc.)

## Context

We require continuous or near-continuous thrust that “hums” rather than detonates, high specific impulse, and compatibility with long-duration human habitation. Chemical and pure fission options are insufficient. Pure antimatter remains impractical. Beamed propulsion is useful as an auxiliary but not sufficient as the sole primary drive for a self-contained Worldship.

## Decision

The primary propulsion system shall be high-repetition-rate inertial confinement fusion using deuterium–helium-3 fuel, with exhaust directed by a superconducting magnetic nozzle.

## Consequences

**Positive**
- High exhaust velocity (target 9,000–12,000 km/s)
- Continuous thrust feel at sufficient pulse rates
- Aneutronic-leaning reaction reduces some radiation issues relative to D-T
- Strong heritage from Project Daedalus and subsequent studies

**Negative / Risks**
- High mass ratio still required (staging mandatory)
- ³He sourcing is non-trivial
- Driver energy and nozzle thermal management remain significant engineering challenges
- Pulse rate and reliability over decades must be proven

## Alternatives Considered

- Nuclear pulse (Orion-style) — rejected due to discrete explosions and political/structural issues
- Pure magnetic confinement continuous fusion — still less mature for high-thrust space application
- Bussard ramjet — rejected due to drag and density problems in real interstellar medium
- Pure photon / laser sail as primary — insufficient for self-contained high-mass Worldship

## Follow-up Work

- Detailed performance model and mass fraction analysis
- Pellet and driver subsystem design
- Magnetic nozzle thermal and efficiency analysis
- ³He acquisition and ISRU pathways
