# Technology Readiness & Realism Statement

**Status:** Authoritative program position  
**Owner:** Autonomicity Games Inc.  
**Authority:** Ra-Thor + Permanent PATSAGi Councils under TOLC 8  
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-11

## 1. Purpose

This document records an honest assessment of the current technology readiness of the Daedalus-Skin Eternal Ark concept relative to demonstrated human capability. It exists so that neither humans nor AI systems can accidentally treat the definition baseline as implying near-term constructability.

## 2. What the Definition Phase Achieved

The repository now contains a coherent, physics-constrained, end-to-end engineering baseline for a Reference Seed Vessel (DSA-Ref-A):

- Clear architecture and interfaces
- Quantified mass, power, propellant, and life-support frameworks
- Safety case, FMEA coverage, and risk picture
- Technology maturation gates and flight-test logic
- Industrial and fuel-acquisition prerequisites
- Human systems and long-duration simulation design

This is valuable systems engineering. It is **not** a claim that the required technologies are ready.

## 3. Hard Gaps Relative to Demonstrated Technology

The following remain well beyond what has been demonstrated as of the current date:

| Domain | Current Reality | Gap to DSA-Ref-A Requirement |
|--------|-----------------|------------------------------|
| Sustained D–³He ICF with magnetic nozzle | Laboratory-scale or pulsed experiments; no multi-year continuous or high-duty-cycle space-qualified system | Multi-year reliable operation at ship scale |
| ³He acquisition at relevant scale | Extremely limited terrestrial and lunar demonstration; no industrial gas-giant or large-scale lunar production | Reliable, affordable supply for even one Seed Vessel propellant load |
| Radiation-hardened, self-healing large structures | Materials research and small-scale demonstrations; no multi-decade validated large rotating habitat | 140 m class rotating pressure vessel + Daedalus-Skin with multi-decade integrity |
| Closed-loop life support at 300-person, multi-year scale | ISS and ground analogs at much smaller scale and shorter duration | Stable, dual-path, multi-year closed loop with trace contaminant control |
| Large superconducting magnetic sail | Conceptual and small-scale work | Deployment, stability, and useful drag at interstellar densities |
| Multi-decade reliability of the integrated system | No precedent | Everything must function or be maintainable for decades |

**Scaling remains the long engineering climb.** The definition work does not shorten the fundamental physics, materials, and industrial gaps; it only makes them visible and ordered.

## 4. Program Posture Required by TOLC 8

Because the gaps are real and large:

1. The Seed Vessel is deliberately smaller and lower-performance than a full Worldship precisely to reduce (not eliminate) the climb.
2. No full-scale assembly authorization is permitted until the highest-risk technology threads (especially fusion endurance, rotating habitat mechanics, and closed-loop life support) pass defined maturation gates with real data.
3. Residual fusion propellant is retained as operational backup precisely because magnetic sail performance is uncertain.
4. Industrial capacity and fuel acquisition are treated as parallel programs, not assumed background.
5. All claims of readiness must be backed by test evidence, not by the existence of a design document.

## 5. Correct Use of This Repository

- **Correct:** As a coherent systems baseline, risk register, maturation roadmap, and collaboration framework that keeps work honest and ordered.
- **Incorrect:** As evidence that the ship can be built on near-term timescales or that the key technologies are already solved.

## 6. Councils’ Position

The PATSAGi Councils affirm that truth-seeking requires stating the gaps without inflation or minimization. The architecture is sound within known physics. The engineering climb is long. The repository exists to make that climb legible, gated, and governable under permanent zero-harm constraints.
